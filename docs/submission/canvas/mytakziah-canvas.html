1 Problem

Three agencies. Three forms. The same information, demanded again each time.

A survivor makes at least two mandatory in-person trips, carrying original documents, to two different agencies, to prove the same death.

The Concept
MyTakziah
One death.
Tell government once.
A family gives its evidence a single time. MyTakziah prepares the claims at JPN, KWSP and PERKESO, submits them with consent, and tracks them in one timeline.
A proposed addition to the MyGOV family. Rules are published and cited. The agency still decides.
3 Innovation

A chatbot tells you what to do. MyTakziah does it.

The once-only principle, applied to one life event.

  • Published rules, not a model deciding. Every condition carries its source link.
  • Ranked by deadline, not by value. The EPF window is six months.
  • Consent per agency, shown separately, granted separately.
  • Drafts, never auto-approval. The agency keeps every decision.

The UK's Tell Us Once notifies agencies of a death. MyTakziah claims.

4 Impact
198,992

deaths in Malaysia in 2024. Every one starts the same paperwork.

  • RM3.05 billion in EPF death withdrawals across 85,731 claims, 2023
  • RM2,500 EPF Death Assistance, lost if not claimed within six months
  • Up to RM3,000 PERKESO funeral benefit

What we would measure, and have not yet: information items re-entered per death, mandatory in-person trips, and claims completed inside the six-month window.
