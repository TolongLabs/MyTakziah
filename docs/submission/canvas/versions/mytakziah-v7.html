3. Innovation

A chatbot tells you what to do. MyTakziah does it.

The once-only principle, applied to one life event.

Published rules, not a model deciding. Every condition cites its source.

Ranked by deadline, not by value. The EPF window is six months.

You approve each agency separately. Nothing is sent until you do.

Never auto-approved. The agency decides every claim.

The UK's Tell Us Once notifies agencies of a death. MyTakziah prepares and files the claim.
