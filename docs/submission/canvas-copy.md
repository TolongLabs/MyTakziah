## The Copy

A stranger must get this in five seconds. **30 per cent of the final score is a student popular
vote.** Every word below is load-bearing; cut rather than add.

### The Concept (Centre)

> ## MyTakziah
>
> ### One death. Tell government once.
>
> A family gives its evidence a single time. MyTakziah prepares the claims at JPN, KWSP and
> PERKESO, submits them with consent, and tracks them in one timeline.
>
> A proposed addition to the MyGOV family. Rules are published and cited. The agency still decides.

### 1. Problem

> ### Three agencies. Three forms. The same information, demanded again each time.
>
> | Demanded of the family | JPN | KWSP | PERKESO |
> | ---------------------- | :-: | :--: | :-----: |
> | Who died               | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |
> | Who you are            | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |
> | Proof of the death     | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |
> | How you are related    |  -  | Yes  |   Yes   |
> | Where to pay           |  -  | Yes  |   Yes   |
>
> **A survivor makes at least two mandatory in-person trips, carrying original documents, to two
> different agencies, to prove the same death.**

### 2. Target User

**Use this version if #5 does not happen:**

> ### The surviving spouse, usually older, usually alone with the paperwork.
>
> Median age at death in Malaysia: **69.0 years**.
>
> **91.6%** of Malaysians aged 60 and over use the internet.
> Only **33.6%** of them deal with government online, against 38.9% overall.
>
> **They are connected. They are still the least likely to transact. The barrier is the process,
> not the device.**

**Add this line, from `../research/documented-accounts-bereavement.md`:**

> **One widow collected her PERKESO survivor benefits 23 years late. She was never refused. She
> was never told.**

That single line does more for the Target User pillar than a statistic, and it is sourced. Do not
widen it into a claim about how many families this happens to.

### 3. Innovation

> ### A chatbot tells you what to do. MyTakziah does it.
>
> The once-only principle, applied to one life event.
>
> - **Published rules, not a model deciding.** Every condition carries its source link.
> - **Ranked by deadline, not by value.** The EPF window is six months.
> - **Consent per agency**, shown separately, granted separately.
> - **Drafts, never auto-approval.** The agency keeps every decision.
>
> The UK's Tell Us Once **notifies** agencies of a death. MyTakziah **claims**.

### 4. Impact

> ### 198,992 deaths in Malaysia in 2024.
>
> Every one starts the same paperwork.
>
> - **RM3.05 billion** in EPF death withdrawals across **85,731** claims, 2023
> - **RM2,500** EPF Death Assistance, lost if not claimed within **six months**
> - **Up to RM3,000** PERKESO funeral benefit
>
> **What we would measure, and have not yet:** information items re-entered per death, mandatory
> in-person trips, and claims completed inside the six-month window.

## Design Notes For #13

- **Reading order is Concept first, then left column top to bottom, then right column.** The
  centre must be visually dominant.
- **The Problem table is the centrepiece.** It is the one thing a judge can verify at a glance.
  Give it room. Do not shrink it to fit more words elsewhere.
- **Tone: dignity and administration, never death itself.** No bodies, no funeral imagery, no
  religious iconography, no crying. Show documents, forms, deadlines and repeated fields.
- **No government logos or crests.** Nothing implying MyTakziah is an existing or sanctioned
  MyGOV service. Recorded decision, 2026-08-18.
- **Muted palette.** Text must stay legible at A3 and in a thumbnail, because the popular vote
  is decided on a scrolled feed.
