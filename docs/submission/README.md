# Submission

Everything needed to turn the selected concept into the two required artifacts and verify them before Kolaxus upload.

**Read `../competition/submission-rules.md` first.** The deadline is 21 August 2026 at 11:59 PM, and no extension will be given.

## Required Artifacts

| Artifact          | Format                     | Required Content                                                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Idea Submission   | PDF only, under 25 MB      | Team Information and Sections 1 through 8 from the official template |
| Innovation Canvas | one A3 portrait JPG or PNG | The Concept, Problem, Target User, Innovation, and Impact            |

Optional apps or videos come only after both required artifacts pass the weighted review and preflight. They must strengthen the idea without delaying submission.

## Suggested Working Files

Create these only when the team is ready to fill them with real content:

| File                       | Purpose                                                   |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `idea-submission-draft.md` | master copy for Team Information and Sections 1 through 8 |
| `canvas-copy.md`           | concise five-pillar copy before visual layout             |
| `visual-concept.md`        | notes for Section 7 and the canvas reading order          |
| `references.md`            | source list for factual claims and numbers                |
| `review-notes.md`          | consolidated team comments and requested changes          |

Do not create a polished-looking claim where the evidence is missing. Use `[NEEDS SOURCE]`, `[ASSUMPTION]`, or `TODO:`.

## Idea Submission Map

| Section                    | Core Question                                                              |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Team Information           | who are the 2-5 students and what is the team name?                        |
| 1 The Problem              | what specific problem exists, for whom, and in what context?               |
| 2 Target User              | who experiences the problem most directly?                                 |
| 3 Your Solution            | what does the concept do across the user's journey?                        |
| 4 Innovation Element       | what is distinct and why does it matter?                                   |
| 5 Impact                   | what social or economic change could result, and how could it be observed? |
| 6 Feasibility              | what is the credible path, with dependencies, assumptions, and risks?      |
| 7 Visual Concept           | what should judges see to understand the idea quickly?                     |
| 8 Why Your Team Should Win | why this team and idea deserve selection, in 100 words or less?            |

The questions above are internal drafting prompts. They do not add new official requirements.

## Innovation Canvas Map

The canvas has five pillars:

1. **The Concept:** one clear sentence.
2. **Problem:** the specific need and context.
3. **Target User:** the primary person or group and their situation.
4. **Innovation:** the distinct intervention or approach.
5. **Impact:** the intended social or economic change.

Use short copy, a clear reading order, and only visuals that help judges understand the idea. The final export must be one A3 portrait JPG or PNG.

## Final Sequence

1. Complete the idea-submission draft.
2. Distill the same story into canvas copy.
3. Run the weighted review against `../competition/rubrics.md` and record its outcome in `preflight.md`.
4. Revise both artifacts together.
5. Export the PDF and canvas image.
6. Complete `preflight.md` using the actual exports.
7. Have the human team lead submit through Kolaxus before the deadline.

Email is not an accepted submission channel.
