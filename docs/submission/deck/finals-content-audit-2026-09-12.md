## Overall Verdict

The concept can make a credible finals pitch, but the present deck claims more than its evidence and prototype demonstrate. Its strongest direction is a focused bereavement preparation service, with a connected government journey as a later ambition.

The three most important problems to resolve are:

1. **Product Truth:** Replace filing and integration claims with reviewed preparation and guided official submission. Simulated responses are not agency transactions.
2. **Evidence Precision:** Correct the shared-fields argument, population count and benefit deadline qualifications. Official forms demonstrate repeated information, not universal eligibility, identical documents or the elimination of agency formalities.
3. **Execution Credibility:** Admit missing user validation. Make the community pilot, funding safeguards, team contribution and judge introductions concrete without inventing commitments.

Recommended positioning: **MyTakziah: one family record that prepares the next agency step.**

This direction supports Innovation through a cross-agency preparation workflow, Feasibility through a pre-integration pilot, Impact through measurable preparation outcomes, and Pitch Quality through one clear story. It is not evidence of a validated market or a defensible technical moat.

## Fixed Production Constraints

- Exactly seven slides, in the organiser's seven-section order. No extra cover or appendix slides.
- Six-minute presentation and four-minute Q&A. Target this narration at approximately 5:25, including pauses and transitions.
- White background. At most three main pointers per slide, not three paragraphs disguised as pointers.
- One or two prototype screenshots across the entire deck, plus a QR code. No live or recorded demo.
- Senior business and industry judges. Weights: Innovation 30%, Feasibility 25%, Social or Economic Impact 25%, Pitch Quality 20%.
- No invented facts, validation, partnerships, prices, costs, metrics or feasibility claims.
- Unknowns use `TODO:`, assumptions use `[ASSUMPTION]`, missing evidence uses `[NEEDS SOURCE]`.
- No government endorsement implication. Agencies retain approval and eligibility decisions.
- No em dashes or emoji. Headings and lead labels use Title Case.

## Q&A Bank

### Is This Just Another Chatbot?

The proposed output is a reviewed preparation process: shared information mapped to relevant drafts, supporting documents and next actions. A conversational answer alone does not complete that process. Chatbots can also extract and draft, so our advantage must come from a maintained and tested workflow. We have not yet demonstrated that advantage with users.

### Does MyGOV or MyDigital ID Already Solve This?

MyGOV already brings together services, applications and notifications. MyDigital ID authenticates users. We propose a focused bereavement preparation workflow that could complement them, not replace them. We have no integration agreement and are not claiming that no existing service overlaps.

### Why Not Use MyGDX?

MyGDX is data-exchange infrastructure, not automatic permission for our team to access agency data. A future connected service would need the relevant authorisations, agreements and technical access. The first test does not depend on that access.

### Is This Different From Tell Us Once?

Tell Us Once is important prior art for coordinated death notification. We do not claim to have invented that idea. Our proposed focus is preparing Malaysian benefit applications against the requirements of these agencies. Whether that focus is useful and sufficiently different still needs testing.

### How Can You Submit Without Agency APIs?

The current prototype does not submit anything. In the proposed first service, MyTakziah would help prepare information and documents, and the family would submit through official channels. Only a genuine agency receipt would count as evidence of submission. Automated filing is a later, separately authorised stage.

### What Happens When the AI Is Wrong?

A wrong document or deadline can cause harm. AI would assist extraction and explanation, not invent eligibility rules. Maintained sources and rules would guide the workflow. Uncertain or conflicting information must stop the affected task for checking, with correction and escalation routes. Agencies decide eligibility. These safeguards require testing before real use.

### Why Use AI at All?

Extraction and plain-language explanation are proposed uses worth testing. Date calculations, field mappings and known conditions should use maintained deterministic logic where appropriate. If AI does not improve accuracy or effort, we should keep the simpler manual or rule-based step.

### How Do You Protect Sensitive Family Data?

The present walkthrough is not a live claims backend. Before real-document use, we need a reviewed design for data minimisation, access controls, encryption, retention, deletion and appropriate consent or authority, including information about living relatives. Initial testing should use fictional details. Family documents must never be shared with paid providers. We are not claiming these safeguards are already implemented or legally certified.

### Have You Interviewed Real Users?

There are no completed user interviews recorded in the repository at the audited baseline. We have reviewed official forms and published accounts, which are not product validation. Our next step is to speak with people who have handled the process and observe where preparation breaks down. Update this answer only if the team supplies genuine contemporaneous records.

### Who Pays, and Why?

Our proposed test is a fixed-fee optional provider directory, with core guidance free. A provider would pay for a listing that families voluntarily choose to use, not access to documents or benefits decisions. No provider has committed, no price is validated and willingness to pay is unknown.

### Is Monetising Access to Grieving Families Ethical?

It would not be ethical to exploit their documents, pressure them into purchases or let payment influence benefit guidance. The proposed boundary is a clearly separate optional directory, voluntary contact, no sale of family data and no commission on benefits. If providers will not pay within those boundaries, we need a different funding model.

### How Will You Acquire the First Users?

Through one proposed community host in Petaling Jaya, beginning with opt-in research involving people with past experience. After the readiness gates, the host could offer voluntary referrals into supervised preparation. No host is secured, and we will not obtain a list of grieving families for marketing.

### What Will the Team Do Immediately After the Competition?

Assign owners, approach a host and reviewers, conduct user research, and test fictional preparation scenarios. We would confirm draft-format acceptability and privacy readiness before using real documents. A public launch date depends on those gates, not on winning the competition.

### What Would Prize Money or Judge Support Enable?

The most valuable immediate support is an introduction to one community host, an experienced benefits adviser and a privacy mentor. Any prize funding would support reviewed preparation tests, participant access and necessary tooling under an agreed budget. The RM18,000 is the top-three pool, not a guaranteed team award, so we cannot claim a funded runway.

### Why Can This Team Execute?

We have four students across computing and actuarial science, a working interactive prototype and documented agency research. Two members have prior experience building Layak, whose award is recorded in the team's materials. That is a starting capability, not proof of agency expertise. Named responsibilities and independent review are the next gaps to close.

### Does Provide Once Mean Families Never Bring Originals Again?

No. It means reusing shared input inside the proposed MyTakziah workflow. Agencies may still require originals, certified copies, declarations, signatures, thumbprints or attendance. We must preserve those requirements and confirm acceptable preparation formats.

### How Much Money or Time Will You Save?

We do not know yet. The EPF withdrawal total shows the journey's financial importance, not money lost or money MyTakziah could recover. The pilot must establish preparation time, correction burden and relevant deadline outcomes before we claim improvement.
