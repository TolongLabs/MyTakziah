Encik Ismail's wife passed away on 14 August.

Nobody Tells Him

Three trips. Three forms.

MyTakziah Team Survive 05 / 15Target User
2. Target User

The family member left to administer the death.

Most often an older spouse. The median age at death in Malaysia was 69.0 years in 2024, and the largest single age band was 70 to 74.

91.6% of Malaysians aged 60 and over used the internet in 2024, up from 86.9 per cent in 2023
33.6% of that group interact with government organisations online, against 38.9 per cent overall

They are connected. They are the age group least likely to transact with government online.

The barrier is the process, not the device. A simpler digital front door does not fix a journey that requires three separate in-person submissions of the same evidence.

Source: Department of Statistics Malaysia, ICT Use and Access by Individuals and Households, 2024. Median age at death from Vital Statistics Malaysia 2025.

What The User Actually Needs
1 To give each piece of evidence once.
2 To be told which deadline comes first, not which benefit is largest.
3 To know what each agency will receive before it is sent, and to agree to each separately.
4 To see one status, not three.

This profile is desk research, not primary user research. It is built from the three agencies' published requirements and from national statistics, and we do not present it as user validation.

MyTakziah Team Survive 06 / 15The Solution
3. Your Solution

A family should only have to tell government once.

The family gives its evidence a single time. MyTakziah prepares the claims at JPN, KWSP and PERKESO, submits them with the family's consent, and tracks them in one timeline.

Step 1
Start from a verified death
A verification record already exists from a hospital, PDRM or DBKL. MyTakziah does not verify a death and does not register it. JPN registers the death.
Step 2
Provide once
Who died, who you are, proof of the death, how you are related, where money should go. Five things, entered a single time.
Step 3
See what may be owed
Ranked by deadline, not by value. Every card reads potentially eligible, confirmation required, and links the published rule it came from.
Step 4
Consent, per agency
Separate tabs. Each shows exactly what that agency would receive, each with its own explicit consent action.
Step 5
Drafts and one timeline
Pre-filled forms, then a single consolidated status view across all three agencies, in one place.
Submitted, receipted and tracked. Never approved.

The agency keeps every eligibility and approval decision. MyTakziah pre-fills forms and cites published rules. It does not decide, and it never tells a family it has been approved. If the system is wrong, the cost is a correction, not a family's money.

Deliberately Out Of Scope

Estate distribution, faraid and probate, banks, zakat, and local burial management. Version one covers Peninsular Malaysia, with Sabah and Sarawak, whose registration procedures differ, named as phase two.

MyTakziah Team Survive 08 / 15Innovation
4. Innovation Element

A chatbot tells you what to do. MyTakziah does it.

The once-only principle, applied to one life event, across agency boundaries. Malaysia already has the rails: MyDigital ID for identity, MyGDX for data exchange, the Data Sharing Act 2025 for the legal basis. What it does not have is a journey.

A general government assistant MyTakziah
Generates an answer Cites a published rule and pre-fills a form
Covers every service shallowly Covers one life event completely
The model's output is the product The model never decides eligibility
You still fill the forms The forms arrive filled, for your consent

The innovation is not a new technology. It is refusing to make a grieving family the integration layer between three institutions that already share infrastructure with each other.

The Four Design Choices That Carry It
Deterministic, published, cited rules

Roughly a dozen conditions across three agencies, every one traceable to a government page or form. Provenance is visible to the user.

Ranked by deadline, not by value

The six-month EPF window outranks a larger benefit with no deadline. No existing service does this.

Consent per agency, granted separately

The family sees exactly what each agency receives.

Drafts, never auto-approval

If the system is wrong, the cost is a correction, not a family's money.

02 JPN JPN has already built an AI assistant with a dedicated death module, live on its website, with quick actions for Daftar Kematian and Semak Status. It answers JPN questions well. It cannot tell KWSP anything.
03 MyDigital ID Almost 10 million registrations as of February 2026, and soon required for death certificate services. It proves who you are, once, at each agency's door in turn. It carries nothing between them. It is the layer beneath us, not a competitor.

4. Innovation Element

Four fair challenges, four answers.

These are the questions a judge should ask, and the answers every member of Survive can give from memory.

Is this not the MyGOV chatbot?

A chatbot tells you what to do. This does it. And JPN has already built the chatbot, with a dedicated death module. It still cannot tell KWSP anything.

Is this not the UK's Tell Us Once?

They notify. We claim. Notification stops a family being asked again. It does not put money in their hands, and it does not protect a deadline.

Does MyDigital ID not do this?

The Minister says it is purely verification and authentication. It proves who you are, once, at each agency's door in turn. It is our foundation, not our competitor.

Are they not all at UTC already?

Twelve cities, against close to 199,000 deaths a year. At UTC Perak, JPN and PERKESO sit on Level 3 and KWSP is a ground floor kiosk. You still queue three times.

5. Impact

Every death starts the same paperwork.

None of this is new money. It is already funded and already owed. The gap is whether a grieving family knows to claim it, and claims it in time.

198,992

deaths registered in Malaysia in 2024, up 1.0 per cent from 196,965 in 2023

RM3.24b

in EPF death withdrawals in 2024, across 85,532 transactions

RM2,500

EPF Death Assistance, at EPF's discretion, and lost if not claimed within six months

RM3,000

PERKESO funeral benefit at most: actual expenditure or RM3,000, whichever is lower

What We Would Measure, And Have Not Yet

Core information items re-entered per death. Mandatory in-person trips per death, currently at least two. Claims submitted inside the six-month EPF window. Rate of incomplete or rejected submissions. These are proposed future measures, not results.

Aligned With Two UN Goals, Quoted At Target Level

SDG 16, target 16.6, effective, accountable and transparent institutions at all levels. SDG 1, target 1.3, social protection systems for all. We do not claim SDG 10 or SDG 5, because our recorded scope and our evidence do not support them.

In 2024 the EPF made 27,006 Death Assistance payments against 85,532 death withdrawals. Most death withdrawals carry no Death Assistance payment, and EPF publishes no breakdown of why. Death Assistance also requires the member to have died before age 60, and the median age at death is 69.0, so the age condition accounts for a large share on its own. We name the gap, not a cause for it.

MyTakziah Team Survive 12 / 15Feasibility
6. Feasibility

The rails exist. We are building the journey.

What is already built is not ours to build. What is not yet proven, we name.

Already built, not ours to build Not yet proven, and named as such
MyDigital ID, almost 10 million registrations as of February 2026, and the identity anchor this concept assumes. Public submission and status APIs at JPN, KWSP and PERKESO are unverified. The integration is proposed, not proven.
MyGDX, 81 government agencies across 49 integrated systems and more than 65 million API transactions. The Data Sharing Act 2025 governs sharing between federal bodies. It does not prove production APIs exist, and it does not authorise an agent to transact for a citizen.
The Data Sharing Act 2025, the legal basis for federal bodies to share data with each other. PERKESO eligibility turns on a contribution history no family and no third party can see: 24 monthly contributions in the 40 months before death. It is only ever shown as potentially eligible.
PERKESO Portal LINDUNG FAEDAH already accepts a Death Notice online. One of the three has built an online death channel. PERKESO enforces a claim deadline it does not publish. No number is published anywhere, and we do not invent one.
First Practical Steps
1 Author the deterministic rule set for the three agencies from published sources. Already begun.
2 Validate the shared-fields matrix with one agency counter or a khairat committee.
3 Pilot as pre-filled forms the family carries, which requires no agency integration at all and tests the once-only premise immediately.
4 Only then approach agencies about receiving prepared submissions directly.

Step three is the honest core of the case. The concept delivers value before any integration exists.
