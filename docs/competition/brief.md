# Competition Brief

## Identity

**Competition:** URIIS - Student Biz Innov Challenge 2026

**Tagline:** "Small Ideas. Big Impact."

**Theme:** "Empowering Citizens. Transforming Government. Shaping Malaysia's Future."

**Platform:** Kolaxus

## Goal

The challenge aims to inspire students to create impactful, scalable innovations that drive entrepreneurship, leadership, and nation-building. Top teams pitch live at the University Research & Innovation Investment Summit (URIIS) 2026 in Kuala Lumpur.

## Participation

- Team size: 2-5 students.
- No coding is required.
- No prototype is required.
- No technical expertise is required.

## The Four Tracks

**Pick exactly one.** The presenter states this twice in the participant briefing: "you can choose one of the problem statement" and "Just choose one of the problem statement" (`../source/2026-08-03-participant-briefing-transcript.md`, `[00:15:39]` and `[00:15:47]`).

This rule is not written in either PDF. The briefing deck labels the four as "Idea Sample 1" through "Idea Sample 4", which reads as optional inspiration, so confirm on the Kolaxus forum before betting the submission on a fifth idea of our own.

The four statements below are locked inside an image in the briefing PDF and cannot be extracted as text from it. They are transcribed here and in `../source/README.md`.

### 1. Fostering Trustworthy and Ethical AI in the Public Sphere

How might we develop a transparent, user-centric tool that enables Malaysian citizens to understand, monitor, and provide feedback on AI systems used in public service delivery, thereby building public trust and ensuring accountability?

### 2. One-Stop AI Assistant for End-to-End Government Services

How might university students design an AI-powered conversational assistant that helps citizens and businesses complete government transactions end-to-end through MyGOV, without re-entering the same information across different agencies?

### 3. Future Talent Ecosystem: AI for Employment & Skills

How might we leverage AI to build a future-ready talent ecosystem that bridges the gap between education and employment by improving skills matching, career readiness, workforce planning, and lifelong learning for Malaysians?

### 4. Empowering Rural Communities Through Innovation

How might we harness digital technologies and AI to empower rural communities by improving access to essential services, creating sustainable economic opportunities, and enhancing the quality of life for all Malaysians?
