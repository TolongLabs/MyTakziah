# Judging Rubric

## Weighting

| Criterion              |   Weight | Evidence the Draft Must Make Easy to Judge                                                         |
| ---------------------- | -------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Innovation             |      30% | what is distinct, why the distinction matters, and how the concept improves the current experience |
| Feasibility            |      25% | credible delivery path, dependencies, risks, assumptions, and practical first steps                |
| Social/Economic Impact |      25% | who benefits, what changes, why the change matters, and how impact could be observed               |
| Submission Quality     |      20% | complete sections, clear logic, strong visual concept, concise writing, and compliant files        |
| **Total**              | **100%** | one coherent, evidenced submission                                                                 |

The descriptions in the third column are internal review prompts, not additional official criteria.

## Weighted Review Questions

### Innovation: 30%

- What is the central innovation in one sentence?
- Is it more than a generic use of AI or digital technology?
- Does the innovation respond to a specific target-user need?
- Can the team explain what changes compared with the current experience?

### Feasibility: 25%

- What must be true for the idea to work?
- Which assumptions are labeled?
- What could the team test or implement first?
- Are dependencies and risks acknowledged without overpromising?

### Social/Economic Impact: 25%

- Who benefits directly and indirectly?
- What social or economic outcome is expected?
- Is each factual baseline sourced?
- Are proposed metrics clearly future measures rather than fabricated results?

### Submission Quality: 20%

- Are Team Information and Sections 1 through 8 complete?
- Is Section 8 no more than 100 words?
- Do the PDF, canvas, and visual concept tell the same story?
- Are the exports legible and compliant with every format rule?
