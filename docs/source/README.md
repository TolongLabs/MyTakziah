## Authority

Where sources disagree, the PDFs win. The transcripts are machine generated and
carry transcription errors, particularly on proper nouns, acronyms, and
Malaysian place names. Quote the PDFs in the submission, and use the transcripts
to find things the PDFs do not say.

## Why the transcripts are worth reading anyway

The four problem statements appear in the briefing PDF as an **image**, so they
are not extractable as text from the PDF. The participant briefing transcript
discusses all four in the presenter's own words from roughly `[00:12:35]`
onward, along with the reasoning behind them.

The live Q&A also covers edge cases that appear nowhere in either PDF. If you
have a "are we allowed to..." question, search the transcript before asking.

## The four problem statements

Reproduced here because they are locked in an image in the PDF. Verbatim:

**1. Fostering Trustworthy and Ethical AI in the Public Sphere**
How might we develop a transparent, user-centric tool that enables Malaysian
citizens to understand, monitor, and provide feedback on AI systems used in
public service delivery, thereby building public trust and ensuring
accountability?

**2. One-Stop AI Assistant for End-to-End Government Services**
How might university students design an AI-powered conversational assistant that
helps citizens and businesses complete government transactions end-to-end
through MyGOV, without re-entering the same information across different
agencies?

**3. Future Talent Ecosystem: AI for Employment & Skills**
How might we leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) to build a future-ready
talent ecosystem that bridges the gap between education and employment by
improving skills matching, career readiness, workforce planning, and lifelong
learning for Malaysians?

**4. Empowering Rural Communities Through Innovation**
How might we harness digital technologies and Artificial Intelligence (AI) to
empower rural communities by improving access to essential services, creating
sustainable economic opportunities, and enhancing the quality of life for all
Malaysians?

## Provenance

The two transcripts were generated on 18 August 2026 from the organisers' MP4
recordings using the team's local GPU transcriber (OpenAI Whisper, `small`
model, English). The source MP4s were deleted after transcription was verified
against the full recording duration. Per-file details are in each transcript's
