## Question This Answers

When one death is handled across JPN, KWSP and PERKESO, which items of information does the family have to supply more than once, and to whom?

## The Headline Finding

**Three agencies. Three different forms. The same five categories of core information, demanded again each time.**

| Core Information             | JPN | KWSP | PERKESO | Times Demanded |
| ---------------------------- | :-: | :--: | :-----: | -------------: |
| Identity Of The Deceased     | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |          3 / 3 |
| Identity Of The Claimant     | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |          3 / 3 |
| The Fact Of The Death        | Yes | Yes  |   Yes   |          3 / 3 |
| Relationship To The Deceased | No  | Yes  |   Yes   |          2 / 3 |
| Where To Pay The Money       | No  | Yes  |   Yes   |          2 / 3 |

The sharpest single fact: **JPN issues the death certificate, and the family then carries that same certificate separately to KWSP and to PERKESO, each of which demands it again in its own format alongside its own form.** Nothing carries across.

## What Each Agency Actually Demands

Reproduced from the official pages and forms. Wording is the agency's own.

### JPN, Ordinary Registration Of Death, Peninsular

Documents to be submitted by the informant or next of kin at the NRD office:

1. Copy of Death Register or Burial Permit NRD.LM02 (Pin.1/11), the blue copy.
2. Identity card or identification document of the deceased, original.
3. Identity card or identification document of the informant, original and copy.
4. Medical practitioner's certification of the causes of death, original, Form NRD.LM09 or NRD.LM10 for post-mortem, if the death occurred in hospital.

Form NRD.LM02 is obtained at the NRD counter. There is no fee. Those eligible to collect the death certificate are the deceased's mother or father, husband or wife, children, biological siblings, or the informant recorded in NRD.LM02.

### KWSP, Death Withdrawal

Required documents, as listed by KWSP:

1. Form KWSP 9KM (AHL) and checklist.
2. Copy of identification documents with original for verification.
3. Copy of applicant's birth certificate with original for verification, if required.
4. Member's death certificate, or the nominee's death certificate if the nominee has passed away.
5. Member's birth certificate if the member is unmarried, or marriage certificate if married, or divorce certificate or proof of relation to the applicant if divorced.
6. Bank account details: savings account statement, current account statement, a verification letter of account holder's details from the bank, or the estate account of the deceased under the deceased's name.
7. Declaration of marriage letter, if the member is a non-Muslim married before 1 March 1982.
8. Power of attorney, meaning an official letter issued by the courts, legal institutions, or Amanah Raya Berhad.
9. Recipient's declaration of responsibility, Muslims, Appendix A.
10. Recipient's declaration of responsibility, non-Muslims, Appendix B.

### PERKESO, Borang 34 Document Checklist, Section D, Notis Kematian

From the official "Senarai Semak Dokumen Permohonan Borang 34":

**Main documents.** Borang 34, "Butiran Notis Dan Tuntutan Faedah", plus a copy of the Insured Person's identity document, front and back.

**Death notice supporting documents.**

- Copy of the Insured Person's death certificate or burial permit.
- Receipts for funeral expenses, where the claimant is someone other than the widow or widower, parents, or child or adopted child.
- Relationship evidence, applied in tiers:
  - **Tier A**, if the Insured Person leaves a widow, widower or child: copy of the marriage certificate or marriage register, copy of the widow's or widower's identity document, and copy of the children's birth certificates or identity cards, or adoption confirmation.
  - **Tier B**, if no Tier A but the Insured Person leaves parents and siblings: copy of the Insured Person's birth certificate, copy of the parents' identity documents, and copies of the siblings' birth certificates or identity cards.
  - **Tier C**, if no Tier A or B but the Insured Person leaves grandparents: copy of the Insured Person's birth certificate, copy of the parents' death certificates, copy of the parents' birth certificates, and copy of the grandparents' identity documents.

**Additional documents.** Proof of the private bank account of the Insured Person or the claimant.

**Submission.** At a PERKESO office counter, by post to the nearest PERKESO office, or by other methods PERKESO accepts. PERKESO reserves the right to reject applications that are incomplete, unclear, or later than the stipulated period.

### PERKESO, The Second Checklist: Borang 24 And Borang 26

PERKESO also publishes a separate "Senarai Semak Dokumen Tuntutan Faedah Orang Tanggungan dan Pengurusan Mayat", which uses different forms again:

- **Borang 24**, "Borang Tuntutan Bagi Pencen Penakat / Faedah Orang Tanggungan", the survivors pension and dependants benefit claim.
- **Borang 26**, "Borang Tuntutan Faedah Pengurusan Mayat", the funeral management benefit claim.
- **Borang BKA-1** plus a copy of the applicant's bank book from an approved bank, where applicable.

Supporting documents on this route:

- Copy of the Insured Person's death certificate.
- Copy of the Insured Person's identity card, front and back.
- If the Insured Person was unmarried: copy of the Insured Person's birth certificate; copies of the parents' or grandparents' identity cards where applicable; copies of siblings' birth certificates or identity cards where applicable.
- If the Insured Person was married: copy of the widow's or widower's identity card; copy of the marriage registration certificate or sijil nikah; **Borang Akuan Balu Tunggal**, the sole widow declaration for Muslims, Lampiran A; copies of children's birth certificates or identity cards where applicable.
- Original burial receipts for the expenditure incurred, where there is no next of kin.

**The instruction that matters most on this form**, reproduced from the checklist: "PEMOHON DIKEHENDAKI HADIR DENGAN MEMBAWA DOKUMEN ASAL SEMASA MENGEMUKAKAN TUNTUTAN", meaning the applicant is required to attend in person, bringing the original documents, when submitting the claim.

Read that alongside JPN, which also requires physical attendance at an NRD office. **A survivor makes at least two mandatory in-person trips carrying original documents, to two different agencies, to prove the same death.** That is the once-only failure stated as a journey rather than as a table, and it is the line the video and the canvas should carry.

## The Tier Cascade, Worth Its Own Line In The Submission

PERKESO's relationship evidence is tiered. A family that does not fit Tier A must produce documents about people other than the deceased. A family in Tier C must produce the parents' death certificates and birth certificates as well as the grandparents' identity documents.

So the further a survivor sits from the standard spouse-and-children shape, the more documents about more people they must assemble, at exactly the moment they are least able to. That is a precise, sourced, non-emotive statement of the burden.

## What We May Not Claim

Issue #3 and issue #4 both warn against this, and the matrix confirms the warning was right.

- **Do not claim the three agencies demand an identical document set.** They do not. The forms are entirely different: NRD.LM02, KWSP 9KM (AHL), and Borang 34. The document lists differ in length, in conditionality and in format.
- **The defensible claim is repetition of core information.** The deceased's identity, the claimant's identity and the fact of the death are demanded at all three. Relationship and payment destination are demanded at both benefit agencies.
- **Do not describe this as an integration failure by any named agency.** Each agency's list is reasonable on its own terms. The gap is between them, and that is the point.

## Scope Boundary

This matrix covers **Peninsular Malaysia only**. JPN's death registration procedure for Sabah and Sarawak differs and was not examined. State this boundary in the submission rather than leaving it open, per issue #2.

## Implications For The Concept

1. **Screen 2 of the prototype writes itself.** The "provide once" screen collects exactly five things: who died, who you are, proof of the death, how you are related, and where money should go. Every one of those is demanded at least twice today.
2. **The canvas centrepiece is this matrix, reduced.** Five rows, three columns, ticks. A judge reads it in five seconds without a caption.
3. **The once-only claim is now evidenced, not asserted.** We can name the exact repeated items and cite the form each came from.
4. **Contribution history is the honest limit.** Both KWSP and PERKESO already hold the membership and contribution records; the family never supplies them. That is precisely why eligibility must be shown as "potentially eligible, confirmation required" and never as approved. See `agency-rule-map.md`.

## Observation, Team's Call Whether To Use It

JPN's English-language service page is only partly translated. Published instructions to a bereaved informant read, in the original, "Copy Death Register/Burial Permit NRD.LM02 (Pin.1/11) that colour biru" and "Sends copy Death Register / Burial Permit NRD.LM02 (Pin.1/11) colour kelabu to NRD that nearby in masa 7 working days".

This is a factual, checkable accessibility observation, and it supports the argument that the burden is in the interface rather than in the policy. It could also read as mocking a government agency, which would cost more than it gains. **Recommendation: do not put it in the PDF or the canvas.** Keep it in reserve as an answer if a judge asks whether the problem is really that bad.
